## M26-L01 — Evaluate retrieval and grounded answers

**Delivery:** planned · full lesson contract: not_met · interaction: none · artifact: none

**Prerequisites:** [M25-L04](M25.md#m25-l04), [M17-L04](M17.md#m17-l04), [M21-L04](M21.md#m21-l04)

**Learning outcome:** Separate retrieval failures from unsupported generation.

**Topics:** recall; ranking; answer correctness; faithfulness; citation support; answerability.

**Study estimate:** 2–3 hours guided study + 3–5 hours exercise. These are learner-effort estimates, not an implementation schedule.

**Estimate basis:** Study evaluate retrieval and grounded answers; build or analyze: Implement a decomposed RAG scorecard with independent retrieval and answer labels. Verify: Include correct-but-unsupported and supported-but-incomplete answers. Stated prerequisites met; small local fixture or explicitly labeled replay; excludes large training runs and cloud provisioning. Confidence: low; editorial estimate, not learner-measured.

### Primary readings

- [SEARCH: Sentence Transformers retrieve and rerank](https://sbert.net/examples/sentence_transformer/applications/retrieve_rerank/README.html) — Bi-encoder candidates and cross-encoder ranking.
- [EVAL: OpenAI evaluation best practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) — Task-specific datasets, graders, human calibration, continuous evaluation.

### Interview question

Can a faithful answer still be wrong for the user's question?

**A strong answer addresses:** Evidence correctness; relevance; completeness.

Score 0–3 using the [shared interview rubric](../README.md#interview-rubric).

### Production exercise

Implement a decomposed RAG scorecard with independent retrieval and answer labels.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Include correct-but-unsupported and supported-but-incomplete answers.
- Attribute failures to pipeline stages.

**Evidence to submit:**

- Versioned artifact or design specification.
- Baseline or hand-checkable fixture.
- Results or expected-results table including a failure case.
- Decision and limitations note.

**Resources:** CPU/local fixtures or a mocked provider; live service use is optional.

Submit every lesson artifact, answer its interview question, and explain one failure-and-recovery case. A reviewer checks acceptance criteria and labels results as designed, simulated, replayed or measured. A plan or replay is not proof of production performance.
