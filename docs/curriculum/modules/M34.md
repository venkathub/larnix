## M34-L01 — Collect meaningful preferences

**Delivery:** planned · full lesson contract: not_met · interaction: none · artifact: none

**Prerequisites:** [M33-L04](M33.md#m33-l04), [M03-L03](M03.md#m03-l03)

**Learning outcome:** Define preference labels that reflect task quality rather than style.

**Topics:** pairwise data; chosen and rejected outputs; rubric design; annotator bias; disagreement.

**Study estimate:** 2–3 hours guided study + 3–5 hours exercise. These are uncalibrated learner-effort estimates, not measured timings or an implementation schedule.

### Primary readings

- [TRL: Hugging Face TRL](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/index) — SFT, reward modeling, DPO, GRPO, distillation.
- [EVAL: OpenAI evaluation best practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) — Task-specific datasets, graders, human calibration, continuous evaluation.
- [INSTRUCT: Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155) — Demonstrations, preference labels and RLHF stages.

### Interview question

Why can a preference dataset reward longer but less useful answers?

**A strong answer addresses:** Presentation bias; annotation rules; confounding.

Score 0–3 using the [shared interview rubric](../README.md#interview-rubric).

### Production exercise

Conduct a blinded preference-labeling study for concise, evidence-supported policy answers.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Randomize order.
- Audit disagreements and separate factual errors from stylistic preference.

**Evidence to submit:**

- Versioned artifact or design specification.
- Baseline or hand-checkable fixture.
- Results or expected-results table including a failure case.
- Decision and limitations note.

**Resources:** Tiny/local model, synthetic media, or supplied recorded outputs/profiles; larger GPU or paid-provider runs are optional. Replay analysis cannot establish real hardware performance.

## M34-L03 — Understand policy optimization

**Delivery:** planned · full lesson contract: not_met · interaction: none · artifact: none

**Prerequisites:** [M34-L02](M34.md#m34-l02)

**Learning outcome:** Identify which training signals drive a policy update.

**Topics:** PPO; GRPO; rollout sampling; advantages; clipping; reward normalization.

**Study estimate:** 3–5 hours guided study + 5–9 hours exercise. These are uncalibrated learner-effort estimates, not measured timings or an implementation schedule.

### Primary readings

- [TRL: Hugging Face TRL](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/index) — SFT, reward modeling, DPO, GRPO, distillation.
- [PPO: Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347) — Clipped policy optimization and rollout objectives.
- [GRPO: DeepSeekMath](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03300) — Group relative policy optimization and mathematical reasoning.

### Interview question

Why can rising reward coincide with worsening actual quality?

**A strong answer addresses:** Reward misspecification; exploitation; distribution shift.

Score 0–3 using the [shared interview rubric](../README.md#interview-rubric).

### Production exercise

Run or replay a tiny rollout experiment for verifiable arithmetic tasks, including a deliberate weak-reward exploit.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Trace rewards to advantages and updates.
- Include an exploit that passes a weak reward function.

**Evidence to submit:**

- Versioned artifact or design specification.
- Baseline or hand-checkable fixture.
- Results or expected-results table including a failure case.
- Decision and limitations note.

**Resources:** Tiny/local model, synthetic media, or supplied recorded outputs/profiles; larger GPU or paid-provider runs are optional. Replay analysis cannot establish real hardware performance.

**Learning outcome:** Release an aligned model only after independent task and risk evaluation.

### Primary readings
