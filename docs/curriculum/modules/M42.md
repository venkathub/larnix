## M42-L01 — Explain concepts with tradeoffs

**Delivery:** planned · full lesson contract: not_met · interaction: none · artifact: none

**Prerequisites:** [M41-L04](M41.md#m41-l04)

**Learning outcome:** Answer conceptual questions with mechanism, example and limitation.

**Topics:** retrieval; tuning; inference; agents; statistics; concise explanations.

**Study estimate:** 1–2 hours guided study + 2–3 hours exercise. These are learner-effort estimates, not an implementation schedule.

**Estimate basis:** Study explain concepts with tradeoffs; build or analyze: Record a mock interview using ten questions drawn across the curriculum. Verify: Use a rubric for correctness and clarity. Stated prerequisites met; small local fixture or explicitly labeled replay; excludes large training runs and cloud provisioning. Confidence: low; editorial estimate, not learner-measured.

### Primary readings

- [RAG: Retrieval Augmented Generation paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401) — Combining parametric generation with retrieved evidence.
- [PEFT: Hugging Face PEFT](https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/index) — Adapters, efficient tuning, quantization integration.
- [EVAL: OpenAI evaluation best practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) — Task-specific datasets, graders, human calibration, continuous evaluation.

### Interview question

Explain RAG versus fine-tuning for a changing policy assistant in two minutes.

**A strong answer addresses:** Knowledge versus behavior; freshness; evidence; cost.

Score 0–3 using the [shared interview rubric](../README.md#interview-rubric).

### Production exercise

Record a mock interview using ten questions drawn across the curriculum.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Use a rubric for correctness and clarity.
- Replace memorized slogans with a worked example and counterexample.

**Evidence to submit:**

- Versioned artifact or design specification.
- Baseline or hand-checkable fixture.
- Results or expected-results table including a failure case.
- Decision and limitations note.

**Resources:** CPU/local fixtures or a mocked provider; live service use is optional.

Submit every lesson artifact, answer its interview question, and explain one failure-and-recovery case. A reviewer checks acceptance criteria and labels results as designed, simulated, replayed or measured. A plan or replay is not proof of production performance.
