## M13-L01 — Parse heterogeneous documents

**Delivery:** planned · full lesson contract: not_met · interaction: none · artifact: none

**Prerequisites:** [M06-L04](M06.md#m06-l04), [M11-L03](M11.md#m11-l03)

**Learning outcome:** Define a canonical document representation with source coordinates.

**Topics:** PDF; HTML; office files; OCR; layout; reading order; encodings.

**Study estimate:** 2–3 hours guided study + 4–6 hours exercise. These are uncalibrated learner-effort estimates, not measured timings or an implementation schedule.

### Primary readings

- [DOC: Docling documentation](https://docling-project.github.io/docling/) — Document parsing, layout, tables, OCR, structured representations.

### Interview question

Why does clean-looking extracted text still break downstream retrieval?

**A strong answer addresses:** Reading order; missing tables; headers; source loss.

Score 0–3 using the [shared interview rubric](../README.md#interview-rubric).

### Production exercise

Create original scanned-form, HTML-policy and table-heavy manual fixtures; parse them into a canonical representation.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Preserve page and section provenance.
- Report parsing failures instead of silently dropping content.

**Evidence to submit:**

- Versioned artifact or design specification.
- Baseline or hand-checkable fixture.
- Results or expected-results table including a failure case.
- Decision and limitations note.

**Resources:** CPU/local fixtures or a mocked provider; live service use is optional.

## M13-L04 — Operate ingestion pipelines

**Delivery:** planned · full lesson contract: not_met · interaction: none · artifact: none

**Prerequisites:** [M13-L03](M13.md#m13-l03)

**Learning outcome:** Design idempotent ingestion with explicit failure recovery.

**Topics:** incremental updates; deduplication; deletion; quarantine; reprocessing.

**Study estimate:** 2–3 hours guided study + 4–6 hours exercise. These are uncalibrated learner-effort estimates, not measured timings or an implementation schedule.

### Primary readings

- [DATA: Hugging Face Datasets](https://huggingface.co/docs/datasets/index) — Loading, mapping, streaming, dataset processing.
- [ML: Made With ML course](https://madewithml.com/courses/mlops/) — Product and systems design, data, training, tracking, tests, versioning, operations.

### Interview question

How do you avoid duplicate chunks after an ingestion retry?

**A strong answer addresses:** Stable identity; versioned writes; atomic publication.

Score 0–3 using the [shared interview rubric](../README.md#interview-rubric).

### Production exercise

Build an idempotent ingestion prototype and inject a failure halfway through a policy update.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Expose only complete versions.
- Verify deletion propagation and safe replay.

**Evidence to submit:**

- Versioned artifact or design specification.
- Baseline or hand-checkable fixture.
- Results or expected-results table including a failure case.
- Decision and limitations note.

**Resources:** CPU/local fixtures or a mocked provider; live service use is optional.
