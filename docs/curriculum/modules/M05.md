## M05-L01 — Trace a learning step

**Prerequisites:** [M03-L02](M03.md#m03-l02), [M04-L01](M04.md#m04-l01)

**Learning outcome:** Follow one batch from inputs through gradients to parameter updates.

**Topics:** tensors; broadcasting; autograd; losses; backpropagation.

**Study estimate:** 1–2 hours guided study + 2–4 hours exercise. These are learner-effort estimates, not an implementation schedule.

### Primary readings

- [PT: PyTorch learning basics](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/basics/intro.html) — Tensors, data loaders, autograd, optimization, persistence.

### Interview question

Why might gradients accumulate accidentally across batches?

**A strong answer addresses:** Optimizer lifecycle; zeroing; graph retention.

Score 0–3 using the [shared interview rubric](../README.md#interview-rubric).

### Production exercise

Implement a tiny neural classifier and inspect one forward/backward pass, tensor shapes and parameter updates.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Audit tensor shapes and gradient resets.
- Overfit a tiny fixture before scaling.

**Evidence to submit:**

- Versioned artifact or design specification.
- Baseline or hand-checkable fixture.
- Results or expected-results table including a failure case.
- Decision and limitations note.

**Resources:** Tiny/local model, synthetic media, or supplied recorded outputs/profiles; larger GPU or paid-provider runs are optional. Replay analysis cannot establish real hardware performance.
