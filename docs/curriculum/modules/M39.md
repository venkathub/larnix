## M39-L01 — Understand generative model families

**Delivery:** planned · full lesson contract: not_met · interaction: none · artifact: none

**Prerequisites:** [M05-L04](M05.md#m05-l04), [M25-L04](M25.md#m25-l04), [M31-L03](M31.md#m31-l03)

**Learning outcome:** Explain training and generation at a conceptual level without treating all models as interchangeable.

**Topics:** VAEs; GANs; diffusion; latent diffusion; flow-based generation; conditioning; flow matching.

**Study estimate:** 2–4 hours guided study + 3–5 hours exercise. These are uncalibrated learner-effort estimates, not measured timings or an implementation schedule.

### Primary readings

- [DIFF: Hugging Face diffusion course](https://huggingface.co/learn/diffusion-course/en/unit1/1) — Noise schedules, denoising, conditioning, generation.
- [FLOW: Flow Matching for Generative Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02747) — Continuous flow models, probability paths and flow-matching objectives.

### Interview question

Why does iterative denoising have a different latency profile from one-pass generation?

**A strong answer addresses:** Sampling steps; latent space; architecture.

Score 0–3 using the [shared interview rubric](../README.md#interview-rubric).

### Production exercise

Create a model-family comparison and implement a tiny noise/denoising demonstration.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- State what the toy example omits.
- Separate training objective from sampling procedure.

**Evidence to submit:**

- Versioned artifact or design specification.
- Baseline or hand-checkable fixture.
- Results or expected-results table including a failure case.
- Decision and limitations note.

**Resources:** Tiny/local model, synthetic media, or supplied recorded outputs/profiles; larger GPU or paid-provider runs are optional. Replay analysis cannot establish real hardware performance.
